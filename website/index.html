Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Don't Wake Dad ("the Game") is developed by Michael Carrihill. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Data We Collect

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA) — only with your permission on iOS 14+
  • Approximate location (country/region)
  • Device type, OS version, and ad interaction data

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.

Game progress (stars, run count) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted.

In-App Purchases

The Game offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.
