## Description (4000 chars max)

It's midnight. You need a glass of water. Dad is asleep.

Can you sneak through the house without waking him?

Don't Wake Dad is a tense, procedurally generated stealth game where every run is different. Navigate from your bedroom through the entire house to complete your mission — then get back to bed before Dad wakes up.

**SNEAK**
Tap to move. Walk slow to stay quiet. Run and the noise meter fills fast. Every footstep matters.

**HIDE**
Furniture glows when you're close. Duck behind the couch, under the table, into the closet — and hold your breath while Dad searches.

**SURVIVE**
When Dad wakes up, the whole world turns red. He's faster than you. Your only escape is a hiding spot. Find one — fast.

**EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT**
Random house layouts. Random missions. The kitchen tonight, the living room tomorrow. Complete your objective and sneak back to bed to earn stars.

**FEATURES**
• Procedurally generated house — new layout every run
• 11 different midnight missions
• Full Dad AI — sleeping, stirring, chasing, searching
• Deep scary voice (very low-pitched with reverb)
• Red-screen jump scare when he wakes
• 3-star rating system based on how quiet you were
• Noise meter with heartbeat that rises with danger
• Difficulty scales each run — he gets faster

**REMOVE ADS**
One-time purchase to play completely ad-free. Optional.
